How to Use the IPI Partner Quoting Tool
Everything you need to build and share professional quotes
🚀 Unified Workflow (Single Session)
  • 1Use Workspace to move between Launchpad, Quoting Tool, and Partner Price List. You stay in one contextual session.
  • 2The header context bar shows your active workspace, partner profile, and quote reference so you always know what session you are in.
  • 3Partner profile scope: the Partner Model + Tier in the context bar act as one global profile for both Quoting Tool and Partner Price List during your current session.
  • 4Workspace-specific settings: catalogue category/filter/search and price-list filter/search are remembered per workspace, including when you switch views or reload.
  • 5Add an End Customer, maintain your Quote Reference, and save versions as normal.

📋 Quote Organisation
  • 1Your quote is automatically grouped into Recurring Subscriptions and One-Off Charges, then sub-grouped by product family (CCaaS, PCI Cloud, Services etc.).
  • 2Use Notes & Terms (below the line items) to add covering text, special terms, or a message that will appear on the printed/emailed quote.
  • 3The footer shows live Recurring subscription total, One-Off charges, and ACV (Annual Contract Value = Recurring×12 + One-Off) totals.
⚡ Quick-Start Templates
  • 1Click Quick-Start Templates above the product list to choose a pre-built bundle — e.g. "50-Seat CCaaS Voice Starter" or "AI-First Contact Centre".
  • 2Applying a template to an empty quote loads all products at once. Applying to an existing quote merges the template in, incrementing quantities on any shared products.
🗂️ Saving & Version History
  • 1Click Save v1 to save your first version. A prompt asks for an optional change note — what's in this version?
  • 2After making changes, click Save v2 (the button label updates automatically) to save a new version without overwriting the previous one.
  • 3Click Saved Quotes to see all quotes. Each quote shows its full version history — expand a quote to see v1, v2, v3 etc. Load any version back into the builder.
  • 4Use Copy to duplicate a quote under a new reference — useful for creating variants at different seat counts or configurations.
  • 5Track deal progress with the status dropdown: Draft → Sent → Won / Lost. The pipeline summary at the top of Saved Quotes shows your total ACV by stage.
📤 Exporting & Sharing
  • 1Click Export Quote (or Preview & Export in the footer) to open the export panel with a formatted preview matching the quote layout.
  • 2Print / Save PDF — opens a print-ready document with the full grouped layout, list vs partner prices, discount badges, notes, and validity date. Use your browser's "Save as PDF" option.
  • 3Email Quote — compose and send a professional HTML email with the quote summary directly from the tool. Opens your default email client pre-populated, or copies the formatted content.
  • 4Copy CSV — copies a structured spreadsheet-ready version to your clipboard for pasting into Excel or uploading to HubSpot.
💡 Tips & Shortcuts
Resize the catalogue panelDrag the right edge of the product catalogue to make it wider or narrower. Double-click the edge to reset to default width.
Product detail panelClick the ⓘ button on any product to see a full description, specs, partner pricing, and "often paired with" suggestions.
Notes appear on exportsAnything you type in Notes & Terms shows as a formatted block at the bottom of the printed PDF and emailed quote.
Data is stored locallyAll quotes are saved to your browser's local storage — no account needed. Clear browser data will remove saved quotes.
